Storage derivative of the cell, dV/dη (ft²)

T = dV/dη: plan area of the water surface in the cellT1: running sum of the risesT2: running sum of the falls

T has units of area: volume per foot of level, or a width times the cell length. V = V1 − V2, and both pieces curve upward. That shape is what the nested iteration is built on, and Casulli and Zanolli's convergence proof applies to their algorithm under their assumptions. A solver that changes the loop has to show its own evidence. Above the crown T1 = T2, so V stops growing and the level there is simply crown + P/γ.

      What this is

      A fork of EPA SWMM 5.2.4 with one new source file, raspipe.c, about 2,500 lines. It replaces the explicit link and node update of dynamic wave routing with a semi-implicit finite volume solve over a mesh of pipe cells. Hydrology, controls, statistics and the .out format are EPA's. Quality routing is EPA's with one change: it is told the flow at each end of a conduit.

      SWMM6 on this page

      Version 1.3 adds SWMM6 modified DW and SWMM6 FV as their own lines on the Run and Profile tabs, and a shared table on Verify. The engine is OpenSWMM 6.0.0-alpha.4 from swmm6_rel at 3c30f1e, October 17, 2026, the same build as the Seven SWMM Engines app. OpenSWMM Engine, Copyright 2026 HydroCouple Developers, Apache License 2.0, with public domain EPA SWMM material.       What changed in 1.4

      • Checked against HEC-RAS 7.0.2 on four of HEC's own EXTRAN example plans. The Verify tab opens with the results, and the EXTRAN 1 sample draws RAS's stored result beside the engines.
      • Engine 1.3 adds RASPIPE_PRESSURE_AREA: storage above the crown of a full pipe cell, which HEC-RAS turned out to keep. Off by default, and with it off the engine's .out is the same as 1.2's, byte for byte.
      • A Storage in a full pipe cell control on the Run tab, three cases that reproduce the HEC-RAS figures, and three new entries in the defect register.
      • The Doc now opens with what is unique about the app, the essay in three sizes and four questions, and closes with a measured complexity box. A technical summary in Markdown ships beside the page.

      What changed in 1.2

      • The full equations now use HEC's Eulerian-Lagrangian advection, and momentum crosses a junction of two conduits. Diffusion wave is unchanged, byte for byte.
      • Three outside benchmarks: Stoker's dam break, a U-tube, and the wave scored one cause at a time.
      • A finished run is one record. The chart, the CSV and the saved .inp all read it, and the app says when the controls have moved on.
      • The report and the app give the mesh achieved, the pipe steps, the largest Courant number and the build.
      • A Profile tab, cases that reproduce the published numbers, and a label on every test saying what kind of evidence it is.
      Not HEC-RAS. This is an independent implementation written from HEC's public manuals and the journal papers they cite. It contains no HEC-RAS code. Its results have been compared with HEC-RAS 7.0.2 runs of HEC's EXTRAN examples, and they differ: see Verify. The Hydrologic Engineering Center and the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers did not make, review or endorse it.

        Turn it on

        One word in [OPTIONS]. Without it the engine is EPA 5.2.4, byte for byte.

        FLOW_ROUTING          RASPIPE
RASPIPE_EQUATIONS     DW      ; DW or SWE
RASPIPE_CELL_LENGTH   100     ; ft or m
RASPIPE_MAX_CELLS     20
RASPIPE_THETA         1.0     ; 0.5 to 1.0
RASPIPE_COURANT       0.9     ; 0 = off
RASPIPE_INITIAL_STAGE YES
RASPIPE_WARMUP        0       ; steps, 30 or more
RASPIPE_PRESSURE_AREA 0       ; ft2 per full cell, 1.3
RASPIPE_NESTED_ONLY   NO      ; for testing

        How to use SWMM5 RASPIPE

        1. Run a model. A sample has already run. Pick another in the Model list or load your own .inp.
        2. Change the mesh. Move Cell length and press Run. Long pipes show the biggest change. Target Courant, Warm up and Project stage are the HEC-RAS time step and start-up options. Storage in a full pipe cell sets how much water a pressurized pipe can hold above its crown.
        3. Read the lines. Amber is EPA 5.2.4, blue is RASPIPE, violet is SWMM6 modified DW and green is SWMM6 FV. Choose any conduit or node above the chart, or open Profile to see both grade lines between any two nodes at any moment.
        4. Look inside a cell. That tab shows how a pipe pressurizes without a slot.
        5. Check the claims. Verify opens with HEC-RAS 7.0.2 on HEC's own EXTRAN examples, then lists each test, the kind of evidence it is, what was expected and what came out. Reproduce a published case sets every control for you; the three HEC-RAS cases draw RAS's own result on the chart.
        6. Take it with you. Export CSV and Save the .inp of this run both come from the run shown, whatever the controls say now.
        7. Come back here with the ? button in the header.
