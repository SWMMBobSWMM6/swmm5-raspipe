Storage derivative of the cell, dV/dη (ft²)

T = dV/dη: plan area of the water surface in the cellT1: running sum of the risesT2: running sum of the falls

T has units of area: volume per foot of level, or a width times the cell length. V = V1 − V2, and both pieces curve upward. That shape is what the nested iteration is built on, and Casulli and Zanolli's convergence proof applies to their algorithm under their assumptions. A solver that changes the loop has to show its own evidence. Above the crown T1 = T2, so V stops growing and the level there is simply crown + P/γ.

What this is

A fork of EPA SWMM 5.2.4 with one new source file, raspipe.c, about 2,500 lines. It replaces the explicit link and node update of dynamic wave routing with a semi-implicit finite volume solve over a mesh of pipe cells. Hydrology, controls, statistics and the .out format are EPA's. Quality routing is EPA's with one change: it is told the flow at each end of a conduit.

What changed in 1.2

  • The full equations now use HEC's Eulerian-Lagrangian advection, and momentum crosses a junction of two conduits. Diffusion wave is unchanged, byte for byte.
  • Three outside benchmarks: Stoker's dam break, a U-tube, and the wave scored one cause at a time.
  • A finished run is one record. The chart, the CSV and the saved .inp all read it, and the app says when the controls have moved on.
  • The report and the app give the mesh achieved, the pipe steps, the largest Courant number and the build.
  • A Profile tab, cases that reproduce the published numbers, and a label on every test saying what kind of evidence it is.
Not HEC-RAS. This is an independent implementation written from HEC's public manuals and the journal papers they cite. It contains no HEC-RAS code, and it has not been compared with a run of HEC-RAS. The Hydrologic Engineering Center and the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers did not make, review or endorse it.

    Turn it on

    One word in [OPTIONS]. Without it the engine is EPA 5.2.4, byte for byte.

    FLOW_ROUTING          RASPIPE
RASPIPE_EQUATIONS     DW      ; DW or SWE
RASPIPE_CELL_LENGTH   100     ; ft or m
RASPIPE_MAX_CELLS     20
RASPIPE_THETA         1.0     ; 0.5 to 1.0
RASPIPE_COURANT       0.9     ; 0 = off
RASPIPE_INITIAL_STAGE YES
RASPIPE_WARMUP        0       ; steps, 30 or more
RASPIPE_NESTED_ONLY   NO      ; for testing

    How to use SWMM5 RASPIPE

    1. Run a model. A sample has already run. Pick another in the Model list or load your own .inp.
    2. Change the mesh. Move Cell length and press Run. Long pipes show the biggest change. Target Courant, Warm up and Project stage are the HEC-RAS time step and start-up options.
    3. Read the two lines. Amber is EPA 5.2.4, blue is RASPIPE. Choose any conduit or node above the chart, or open Profile to see both grade lines between any two nodes at any moment.
    4. Look inside a cell. That tab shows how a pipe pressurizes without a slot.
    5. Check the claims. Verify lists each test, the kind of evidence it is, what was expected and what came out. Reproduce a published case sets every control for you.
    6. Take it with you. Export CSV and Save the .inp of this run both come from the run shown, whatever the controls say now.
    7. Come back here with the ? button in the header.
